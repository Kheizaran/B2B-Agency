# Course facts: what the course actually teaches

Source: the six chapter transcripts (1.txt to 6.2.txt), read in full on 2026-09-27, and the chapter 1 intro the owner pasted. Anything a DM reply says about the course must be in this file. If a claim is not here, don't make it; say «از خیزران می‌پرسم».

## One-line description (safe to paraphrase)

A no-code course for freelancers, employees, managers and business owners. You map your own work, build your own knowledge base and memory, turn repetitive tasks into skills, connect tools, build your first agents, and end with your own AI team, all personalised rather than copied. It works in **both Claude and ChatGPT**; the instructor says the two overlap about 95%.

## What the course DOES cover (you may say these)

- **No coding.** «اصلا نیازی نیست ما کدنویسی بلد باشیم». Cowork is for non-programmers.
- **Claude and ChatGPT both.** Everything is shown for both; skills move between them.
- **Starter pack** that builds a personal system through an interview; routines for morning, night and weekly review.
- **Skills** for repetitive work; the instructor says skills carry 60–70% of the automation path.
- **Connectors** to Gmail, Google Drive, Google Calendar, Notion, Slack (ready-made, a few clicks). About 1,900+ connectors exist; connect only what you use.
- **Artifacts**: small apps, websites, games, quizzes, documents and interactive dashboards built by asking Claude (demos: a game, a calorie-counter app, a live daily dashboard).
- **Claude Design**: design systems with your brand colours and fonts; landing pages, presentations, Instagram slides and carousels, mobile app screen designs; comment and edit; export to PowerPoint, Google Slides or PDF; simple logo motion. The instructor made the course slides in it in about 10 minutes.
- **Parallel agents and an AI team**, with a judge agent that scores outputs.
- **Safety**: approve agent actions manually at first (the instructor says for the first 3–4 months), then auto with review, then full autonomy only after trust.

## What the course does NOT cover (never promise these)

- **Image generation and rendering** (e.g. architectural renders): not taught.
- **Video generation or editing**: not taught. A short product demo video appears once in a Claude Code demo, and a video connector was added but not completed. Don't sell video.
- **Production app or software development**: not taught. Artifacts make small apps inside Claude; Claude Code and Codex are "for programmers, we don't go there much".
- **WhatsApp and personal Telegram**: no connector. Their content is copied in by hand, so those workflows are not fully automated.
- **Instagram DMs**: shown through an intermediary (Composio), but the instructor later **stopped** it because of account risk, and he advises against auto-posting to Instagram. Don't promise Instagram automation.
- **Company CRM/ERP integration**: needs custom API work from an agency, not the course. That's an agency lead.
- **Ready-made agents or copy-paste prompts**: the course is against them.
- **Support programme**: no community, Q&A or formal support is mentioned in the transcripts. Don't promise any beyond what the owner confirms.

## Useful lines by audience

- **Designers, marketers, page admins**: Claude Design (brand system, carousels, landing pages, slides) + a content skill. Not image generation.
- **Architects, renderers**: the work *around* design (briefs, meeting notes, change logs, proposals, presentations in Claude Design). Not rendering.
- **Clinic, salon, shop owners**: the course case is a beauty clinic: messy Excel cleaned, customer messages sorted, captions in the clinic's voice, aftercare sheets, follow-up lists. WhatsApp/Instagram replies still sent by hand.
- **Managers and HR**: email triage agent with approval, Drive checker, weekly review, team org chart; CRM/ERP only via the agency.
- **Programmers**: part 1 will feel basic; value is in agents, parallel agents, judges and Claude Code in chapter 6.
